## Standards

### Ongoing contributions

* [UN/CEFACT Web Vocabulary](https://vocab.unece.org). United Nations CEFACT Linked data Vocabulary for trade and supply chains.

* [UN/CEFACT Verifiable Trade Documents](https://unvtd.unece.org). Trade Documents (bill of lading, invoice, certificate of origin, ...) representation and schemas aligned with the [International Chamber of Commerce KTDDE Recommendation](https://www.digitalizetrade.org/ktdde).

* [GS1 MSWG Digital Product Passport and Circularity](https://www.gs1.org/sites/gs1/files/docs/gsmp/textiles_and_apparel_dpp_sub_cfp.pdf). The GS1 standards to comply with the EU [ESPR Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ:L_202401781), namely in the apparel sector.

### Contributions finalised

* [GS1 EPCIS 2.0](https://ref.gs1.org/standards/epcis/). The key standard of traceability in supply chains. Linked Data Vocabulary and Web APIs.

* [NGSI-LD Context Information Management](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_gs/CIM/001_099/009/01.02.01_60/gs_cim009v010201p.pdf). The FIWARE standard for capturing and exposing context information as linked data, particularly in Smart Cities.

* [W3C Device Description Repository Simple API](https://www.w3.org/TR/DDR-Simple-API/). The standard API to gather information about the characteristics of the delivery context (device, browser, network, etc.) to facilitate application adaptation to multiple Web devices.

## Digital Product Passport (DPP)

### DPP for electronics

I have worked together with [eReuse](https://ereuse.org/es/) and the Polytechnic University of Catalonia (UPC) on DPP for electronic devices. Obsolete Electronic devices usually end up at big cemeteries in Africa causing a significant pollution problem. The DPP is a tool that fosters recycling, repairing and reusing, while increasing traceability.

Our prototype has shown how different actors such as manufacturer, repairer, refurbisher, recycler, ... could capture and share information using the [DeviceHub](https://ereuse.org/es/device-hub/) tool. We used [Solidity Smart Contracts](https://docs.soliditylang.org/en/latest/introduction-to-smart-contracts.html) to anchor DPPs enhancing data verification and discovery.

[More information](https://blog.iota.org/dpp-for-electronics-iota/)

### DPP for Plastics

I have worked together with [Digimarc](https://www.digimarc.com) on a project intended to demonstrate how the circular economy for plastics could be enhanced by DPPs. There is a growing pollution problem in agricultural facilities related to plastic consumption. However plastics can be recycled into bioplastics, and such a process is a complex one involving multiple stakeholders (farmers, waste managers, recyclers, manufacturers) that need to share data. We leveraged GS1 EPCIS 2.0 standards, decentralized identity and DLT timestamping.

[More information](https://www.digimarc.com/blog/piloting-digital-product-passport-plastic-recycling)

### Data exchange architectures

I have proposed an [architecture and protocol](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/blockchain/articles/10.3389/fbloc.2026.1872355/abstract) that can enable trusted and sovereign data exchang in value chains, including DPP scenarios. It is based on the [Eclipse Dataspace Protocol](https://eclipse-dataspace-protocol-base.github.io/DataspaceProtocol/2025-1-err2/) and the [Activity Streams](https://www.w3.org/TR/activitystreams-core/) protocol of the Web. It endorses self-sovereign identity W3C standards such as [Verifiable Credentials](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model-2.0/) and [Decentralized Identitifiers](https://www.w3.org/TR/did/).

[See also](https://twindev.org/assets/files/TWIN_Whitepaper_Reference_Architecture-V1.0-January-2026-9f2ab8f3019bcaf1b88cdd9cda72210a.pdf)

## Intellectual Property Rights

In this project we have developed tools and applications that allow to transform legal contracts of the music industry (represented using Linked Data) into Smart Contracts, facilitating more transparent relationships and simpler payments among multiple stakeholders (publishers, authors, producers, composers, musicians, CMOs, etc,). The Smart Contract platform used was the [Ethereum Virtual Machine](https://ethereum.org/developers/docs/evm/) with Solidity and the [Smart Contracts for Media ISO standard](https://standict.eu/discussion-groups/blockchain/287/isoiec-21000-23-smart-contracts-media).

[More information](https://blog.iota.org/ipr-management-iota/)
